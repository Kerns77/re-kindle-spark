Help & FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

How does the whole thing work?

Search for your device above, tell us its condition, and you'll get an instant quote — held for 14 days. If you're happy, we'll send you a free tracked postage pack. Post your device, and once we've verified it we transfer payment the same day.

How does GSX price my device?

Every quote is benchmarked against thousands of live listings from every major UK marketplace — Amazon, eBay, BackMarket, Refurbed and direct-site resellers. That means your price is anchored to what the market is really paying for your device today, not a fixed price that might be months out of date.

How do I get paid?

Bank transfer is fastest — usually within a few hours of us verifying your device. We also offer PayPal or cheque. All three options go out the same day the check completes.

What if my device is different from the quote when it arrives?

We'll email you before doing anything. You choose: accept a revised offer, or have the device sent back to you free of charge. No pressure, no penalty, no auto-accept.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Every device we receive undergoes a certified secure data wipe on arrival — the same standard used by insurance companies and government contracts. Your data is destroyed before the device goes anywhere else.

My device isn't in the search list.

We support 30,000+ devices. If it's not showing up, try a shorter search — for example "Galaxy" instead of the full model name, or just "iPhone 13". If you still can't find it, contact us at hello@2wc2.com and we'll add it.
