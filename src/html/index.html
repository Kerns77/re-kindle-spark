Why compliance matters

The marketplaces are tightening. We tighten with them.

Amazon Renewed, BackMarket certified-refurbisher, Refurbed's audit programme — they're not slowing down. Refurbishers without a per-device audit trail are getting suspended quarterly. GSX is the layer between you and that suspension.

Marketplace seller compliance

Amazon Renewed, BackMarket certified, Refurbed, eBay — every channel demands proof of process. GSX stamps every device with IMEI, grade, wipe certificate, channel and timestamp. The audit trail comes with the listing.

Defends: Amazon Renewed status, BackMarket certification, eBay top-rated seller

IMEI & blacklist sourcing

Every device hits the blacklist, FMIP, SIM-lock, Samsung warranty, Apple warranty, Huawei and LG databases before it lands in your stockroom. Stolen, locked or reported devices flagged at receive — never make it to a listing.

Prevents: selling stolen or locked devices, brand-protection takedowns, account strikes

GDPR-grade data wipe

Certified Secure Data Wipe on every phone, tablet, watch and laptop — per-device certificate stamped to the audit trail. If the wipe is questioned by an auditor or a customer, the certificate is there.

Protects against: GDPR liability, data-leak claims, ICO investigation

Provenance, finance-risk & VAT margin evidence

Every device gets an origin check — original retailer, first activation, country of first sale. Catches two risks: silent blacklisting when the original buyer defaults on handset finance, and margin-scheme disqualification on imported stock (post-Brexit the EU no longer counts). One check. Two audit trails.

Prevents: post-sale blacklisting chargebacks, grey-import listings, HMRC margin-scheme challenges on imported stock
The compliance loop, closed
And the audit trail flows straight into your books. Every margin-scheme-eligible sale, every buyback payout, every B2B trade — auto-classified for VAT and pushed into Xero or QuickBooks with the record HMRC wants. Stripe handles consumer payouts. Ebury and Sokin for cross-border trade. The loop that goes device → sale → VAT → books.
