FAQ

Common questions

Can I use Stockroom without PhoneCheck?

Yes. You can add stock manually — every device in the Product Database is one search away, so specs and title fill in for you. PhoneCheck just makes the process zero-keystroke for devices you've checked.

Which marketplaces does Stockroom push to?

eBay, BackMarket, On Buy, Refurbed and Temu at launch. More are being added — Amazon, Reebelo, TikTok Shop and Shopify are high on the list. Tell us which one your business needs most.

Can I edit listings per marketplace?

Yes. The default is one source record pushing identical listings everywhere, but per-channel overrides let you tune title, photos or price where a specific marketplace needs something different.

What happens when something sells?

When a marketplace confirms a sale, Stockroom marks the device sold and pulls the listing from every other channel immediately — no double-selling.

Is the inventory view really read-only?

The main dashboard view is read-only on purpose — safe for staff to reference without fear of accidental edits. Edit mode requires a deliberate action, and edits are logged per user.

Can I export my inventory?

Yes — CSV export of the full inventory at any time, plus API access on higher plans for anyone integrating with their own warehouse or accounting system.

Does Stockroom replace my accounting system?

No — it's an operational inventory tool, not a full accounting package. But it intergrates to QuickBooks, Stripe and Excel for your bookkeeper to work with.
