Flow

Auto-routing and compliance for every device.

Every device that completes PhoneCheck gets routed automatically — by IMEI status, grade, condition and target channel. Audit trail attached. Exception queue for the 3% that need human judgement. The compliance backbone Amazon Renewed and BackMarket want.

  • IMEI-aware, grade-aware, channel-aware routing
  • Audit trail stamped per device
  • Marketplace-ready compliance exports

Provenance

The check that comes after the blacklist check.

Not all working phones are safe to buy — and not all are eligible for the UK VAT Margin Scheme. Provenance verifies original retailer, activation date, warranty and country of first sale. Catches devices at risk of silent blacklisting when the original buyer defaults on handset finance. Evidences the UK-first-sale requirement HMRC wants for margin-scheme eligibility on imported stock.

  • Original retailer, activation date, warranty state
  • Country of first sale — margin scheme evidence layer
  • Finance-risk flags feed Flow routing rules
  • Per-check or bulk-included at your tier
