FAQ

Common questions

Embedded widget or full-page site — which should I choose?

You can use either, or both. Embed the widget on your existing site if you want customers to trade in without leaving your store. Use the full-page version we host for you if you'd rather link out from a social post, email campaign or QR code — no changes to your site needed. Same pricing, same catalogue, same payouts behind both.

Do I need a developer to install the widget?

No. The widget is a single embed snippet that works on Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix and most custom-built sites. We'll help you install it if needed. And if you'd rather skip the embed altogether, the full-page version we host for you is ready to share from day one.

How do I decide what to pay customers?

You pick a market benchmark (a specific competitor or a blended average) and tell us the margin you want. We calculate your buy price automatically and refresh it daily.

What happens when the customer ships the device?

The customer gets free tracked postage, the device arrives at your premises, you inspect and grade it, and you pay out through your normal payment flow. GSX handles the quoting, the customer journey and the communications.

Can I control which products are buyable?

Yes — you choose every device in your buyback catalogue. Add, pause or remove any SKU at any time.

Is this just for phones?

Phones, tablets and wearables are currently supported. Laptops and gaming are being added. Ask us about expanding your catalogue when you're ready.

What does it cost?

We keep pricing simple and tailored to your volume. Book a demo and we'll quote you on the spot.
