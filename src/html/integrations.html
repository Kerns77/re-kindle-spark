The three-layer story

A curated stack. Not a catalogue.

Other platforms hand you a spreadsheet of eight diagnostic tools and tell you to pick one. GSX doesn't. We've chosen the best-in-class option at every layer — and we tell you which one to use based on your stage.

Layer 1

Two diagnostic paths

PhoneCheck runs as standard on every plan — comprehensive mobile diagnostics and certified data erasure, industry-trusted at any scale. Blancco is the enterprise path where certified data destruction is a compliance requirement.

Layer 2

Accounts-agnostic

Xero or QuickBooks — pick your accounts stack. Stripe for consumer buyback payouts. Ebury or Sokin for cross-border trade. Every VAT-classified sale flows through with the audit trail attached.

Layer 3

Marketplace-connected

Nine marketplaces on the roadmap or already live. Push once, sell everywhere. Sale data closes the loop back into Pricer and Stockroom.
