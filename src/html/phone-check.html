FAQ

Common questions

Is this a mobile app?

No — Phone Check is a desktop application that runs on Mac and Windows. You plug the device into the counter computer with a cable and run the check from there. A mobile companion is on the roadmap.

How is the diagnostic engine built?

The underlying diagnostic engine is powered by phonecheck.com, the industry-leading device testing platform. GSX wraps it in a unified retailer workflow — so a single check also hits blacklist, IMEI and warranty databases and pushes the result straight into your Stockroom.

What does a check actually cost?

You buy credits in packs. 1 credit = 1 full device check regardless of whether it's a phone, tablet, watch or laptop. Per-credit pricing is shown in the portal at checkout, and credits don't expire.

Do I need a special cable or rig?

No. For Smartphones and Tablets, a standard Lightning / USB-C / USB cable is all you need. No test rigs, no custom hardware. We can provide additional test rigs for Apple Watches

Which devices are supported?

Smartphones (iPhone and the major Android brands), tablets (iPad and Android), Apple Watch, MacBooks and most mainstream Windows laptops. We add models continuously — tell us if there's a specific device you need prioritised.

Can I see the raw report?

Yes. Every check produces a full component-by-component report accessible inside your GSX portal, alongside the Stockroom record. Printable or exportable.

Is PhoneCheck included in the subscription?

The app itself is free to install with any GSX plan. Individual checks are paid from your credits balance — that way retailers doing 5 checks a week pay a fraction of what a high-volume refurbisher pays.
