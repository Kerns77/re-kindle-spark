Pulse vs the GSX platform — how to choose

Pulse is the data. Live pricing across the mobile device economy, delivered as reports, CSV or API. Priced separately because the buyers are different — insurers and networks don't run trade-in websites.
The GSX platform is the operations. Buyback widget, Flow auto-routing, Pricer per-channel pricing, Marketplace Push, Provenance. Serious refurbishers usually buy both — the platform to run the floor, Pulse to power their pricing team.

Common questions.

How is this different from what refurbishers get inside the GSX platform?

GSX platform customers get Pulse data surfaced in context — when you're pricing a device in Pricer, Pulse tells you what the market is doing right now. Standalone Pulse gives you the dataset to build your own models against — CSV export, API access, historical archives. Serious refurbishers often buy both.

Where does the data come from?

Live scrapes from recyclers and buyback services in each market, cross-referenced against live marketplace listings (eBay, BackMarket, Refurbed, Amazon Renewed, Swappa etc), grade-normalised. Pulse Pro and Enterprise include historical baselines so you can spot trend inflection points.

Can I get a sample before subscribing?

Yes — email hello@gsx.co.uk with your role and the models or regions you care about. We'll send back an anonymised sample cut so you can see the shape of the data before committing.

Do you publish public market reports?

Quarterly — a free "State of the UK Refurb Market" report drops each quarter, downloadable from the /pulse page. That's the honey trap for the Enterprise Data buyer. Sign up for updates via the contact form.

API rate limits and webhooks?

Pulse Pro: 10,000 requests/day read-only, no webhooks. Pulse Enterprise Data: unlimited requests within fair-use, plus webhook events for price movements, new SKUs and regional shifts. SLA-backed.
