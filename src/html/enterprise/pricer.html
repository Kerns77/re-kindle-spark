Pricer

The resale pricing engine for marketplace sellers.

Pricer prices every SKU you sell, on every channel you sell it on. It benchmarks against the right competitive set per platform, tracks marketplace buyboxes, and tells you the exact price to raise, hold or drop to — per channel, per SKU, every day.

The problem Pricer solves

One product. Six channels. Six different right answers.

Why a single price loses you money

A refurbished Galaxy S24 Ultra in "Mint" condition is competing against a different set of sellers on Amazon than it is on BackMarket, Reebelo, Refurbed, eBay, and your direct site. Each platform has different competitors, different grade labels, different buybox rules, and different price elasticity.

Set one blanket price across all six and you lose the buybox on the platforms where you're priced too high — and leave margin on the table on the platforms where you're already cheapest. Pricer treats every channel independently, with grade-normalised matching against the right competitive set, and recommends the exact price to win each lost buybox or capture margin you're already holding.

What Pricer does

Five capabilities, one engine.

Everything below runs on every device you sell, every day. No spreadsheets, no manual matching.

Per-channel benchmarking

Your direct site compares against other resellers' direct sites. Each marketplace compares against the other sellers on that platform. Never cross-channel — because competition isn't.

Grade-normalised matching

Amazon "Excellent", BackMarket "Premium", CEX "Mint" — all mapped to a unified 0–5 grade scale so like is compared with like. Model + storage + grade matching, colour-agnostic.

Marketplace buybox tracking

For every SKU on every marketplace, we track whether you're holding the buybox (within 2% of the lowest competing offer). Win-rate per platform, lost-buybox count, and the exact price needed to retake each one.

Move-up margin capture

The opportunity most resellers miss. Where you're already cheapest, Pricer recommends raising the price toward — but just under — the next competitor. You keep the buybox, you bank pure margin. No volume lost.

Repricing alerts

Where you're priced above the competitor floor with no buybox to defend, Pricer surfaces the SKU and the suggested cut. Catches placeholder prices, stale listings, and forgotten round-numbers.

️

Per-SKU action recommendations

Every line ships with a specific action: "RAISE £510 → £697 (+£187, still cheapest)" or "REPRICE £910 → £640 (BackMarket buybox)". Daily CSV, dashboard, API or pushed straight into your stock system.

How matching works

Grade-normalised, channel-scoped. Amazon "Excellent", BackMarket "Premium", CEX "Mint" all map to a unified 0–5 grade scale. Direct sites only compare against other direct sites; each marketplace only compares against sellers on that marketplace. The customer's own listings are always excluded from the competitor floor.

Matching key: model + storage + grade — colour-agnostic, because for refurbished devices colour rarely moves price, and matching on it would wrongly isolate exclusive colourways. Phantom and out-of-stock placeholder offers are filtered out before any benchmark is set.
