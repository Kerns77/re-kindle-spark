The problem Flow solves

Every device is an operator decision. That's the problem.

Why manual routing kills compliance and margin

On a floor processing 1,000 devices a day, your team is making thousands of routing decisions: which station?, what grade?, which channel?, repair or list?, has the blacklist been checked?, was the wipe certified?. Each decision is a chance to mis-grade, mis-route, or miss a compliance step that gets you suspended from a marketplace.

Operator error compounds. A 2% mis-grade rate at 1,000 devices/day is 20 wrongly-priced listings a day. A 0.5% blacklist miss is 5 stolen-device listings a day that will trigger an Amazon takedown the moment a buyer reports it. A missed wipe certificate is a GDPR case waiting to happen.

What Flow does

Six routing decisions, automated.

Flow runs as the layer between PhoneCheck (verify) and Stockroom (manage). Every device that completes diagnostics gets routed automatically — and every routing decision is stamped to the device's audit trail.

IMEI-aware routing

Every device hits blacklist, FMIP, SIM-lock, Samsung and Apple warranty databases at receive. Locked, reported or blacklisted devices auto-route to the exception lane — never make it to a listing.

Grade-aware routing

PhoneCheck grade plus cosmetic flags drive the routing decision. Mint goes to A-stock + Amazon Renewed. Good goes to BackMarket. Fair goes to direct-site or B2B Trade Zone. No operator picks.

Channel-aware routing

Each marketplace has different rules: Amazon Renewed wants Mint or Excellent, BackMarket accepts Fair, eBay takes anything graded. Flow knows the rules per channel, routes the device to where it earns the most.

Repair vs refurbish decisioning

Cracked screen + working logic board on a £600 device? Routes to repair queue. Same fault on a £80 device? Routes to B2B Trade Zone. The economic decision happens in the routing logic, not in the operator's head.

Compliance audit trail

Every step generates a record: IMEI check timestamp, blacklist result, grade decision, wipe certificate, routing decision, listing channel, sale price. The audit trail is the listing's defence.

Exception handling

Devices that don't fit the rules — borderline grades, unrecognised IMEI, brand mismatches — route to a supervisor queue with full context. Your supervisor handles 20 exceptions a day instead of 1,000 decisions.

What the audit stamp looks like

Every device leaves Flow with a record stamped to its IMEI: received-at, imei-status, blacklist-result, fmip-status, grade, wipe-cert-id, routed-to, listing-id, sale-price, operator. If a marketplace auditor asks, the record is one click away.
