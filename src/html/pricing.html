Common questions.

What's included on the Starter plan?

Starter includes the Buyback Widget, Product Catalogue, Upload Price Sheet, Diagnostics (PhoneCheck), Dashboard, Digital Services and Settings. Everything you need to run a buyback service end-to-end on your existing site. No volume caps — pricing is by capability, not throughput.

How does the billing work?

Most retailers choose quarterly — 10% off, billed every 3 months in advance. It's the best balance of saving and flexibility. You can also pay monthly, or annually (15% off, billed yearly in advance).

Can I cancel any time?

Monthly plans can be cancelled any time with no fees. Quarterly and annual plans are prepaid and run for the term you've paid for, but you can switch off auto-renewal at any point.

Can I move between plans?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and are pro-rated. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

What's the GSX Shopify Storefront?

A prebuilt Shopify store, pre-wired to your GSX buyback widget, Pricer and Marketplace Push. £1,499 setup + £99/mo — includes theme install, catalogue import (up to 500 SKUs), buyback widget embed, Marketplace Push wiring, checkout config and staff training. Live in 5 business days. You pay Shopify's subscription direct. If you ever leave GSX, your storefront stays with you — it lives on Shopify, on your domain.

What's Provenance?

Provenance verifies where a device was first sold, when it was activated, its warranty status and country of first sale. It flags finance-blacklist risk (crucial in UK, since Ofcom split handset finance from airtime in 2020 — devices can be silently blacklisted months after sale) and evidences UK VAT margin scheme eligibility. Bolt on to any plan at £79/mo, or come bulk-included on Refurbisher and Enterprise.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — Starter, Growth and Pro all start with a 14-day free trial. Enterprise and Custom are demo-led. Email hello@gsx.co.uk.

What if my operation is measured in devices per day rather than website quotes?

The Retail plans are priced by capability — you unlock modules as you climb the ladder. There are no quote caps or throughput fees. If you're processing hundreds or thousands of physical devices per day rather than serving customer quotes on your website, that's a Refurbisher or Enterprise conversation. See gsx.co.uk/refurbishers or gsx.co.uk/enterprise.

Do you charge setup fees?

No standard setup fee for Retail Starter, Growth, Pro, Enterprise or Custom plans. Optional bolt-ons like GSX Shopify Storefront (£1,499 setup) and bespoke work are quoted separately as one-off projects. Refurbisher and Enterprise plans include onboarding as part of the custom-quote conversation.
